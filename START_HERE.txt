================================

2. Git add, commit, push origin main. Render Auto-Deploy neu da bat.
3. Mo web, dang nhap (neu ACCESS_PASSWORD da bat).
4. Vao muc 02, chon/keo tha anh tu may: web TU DONG upload toi
   Cloudinary p8nrkuot (preset harin_unsigned) va luu URL HTTPS.
5. Moi anh hien "URL" la xong. Neu "LOI", bam "Tai lai anh loi"
   ngay o muc 02. Khong can muc cloud config tren web.
